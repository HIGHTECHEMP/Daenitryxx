How the fee is calculated

No city-to-city pricing is required.

1. ROUTELive driving distancePickup and receiving addresses are geocoded and routed.
2. DISTANCEKM × distance rateThe matching kilometre band determines the rate.
3. WEIGHTKG × weight rateThe matching weight band determines the rate.
4. TOTALBase + distance + weightThe minimum fee is applied when necessary.
