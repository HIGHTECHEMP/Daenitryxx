FAQ

Questions, answered.

How do I create a delivery?

Create a customer account, sign in and use the customer dashboard to submit a delivery request.

Can I track a shipment without signing in?

Yes. The public tracking page accepts a valid tracking number and exposes only the shipment information intended for public tracking.

How are drivers approved?

Driver/agent applications are reviewed by administrators. Only approved drivers can receive delivery assignments.

Are payments verified?

Payment verification is designed to happen server-side through a Supabase Edge Function. No provider secret belongs in the browser.

How do I contact support?

Use the Contact Us page or the support form available to signed-in customers.
